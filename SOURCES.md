# Manager Blueprint — content and framework sources

## Product design basis

The application follows the supplied Manager Blueprint PRD. The Blueprint is the central experience and organizes development around Managing Yourself, Managing Others, and Managing Up. Each capability uses a Learn / Apply / Practice / Assess flow. Tools, templates, coaching, and resources are embedded in that capability context rather than treated as separate products.

## Publisher resources used as the initial knowledge-library starting point

The app contains a curated list of external publisher resources with original short summaries and links. It does not scrape, mirror, or ingest full article text. Users open the resources at the publisher's site. The short summaries are Manager Blueprint signposts and do not replace or reproduce the publisher's content.

## Management-style reflection

The 12-question reflection is original. Its behavioral-style labels are informed by public descriptions of DISC-style concepts, while its second set of questions uses broad contribution domains. It is not a validated psychometric instrument and does not reproduce official DiSC or CliftonStrengths question banks or issue official assessment results.

## Management-style background references

The original 12-question management-style reflection uses these public sources as conceptual background only:

- Tony Robbins DISC overview — https://www.tonyrobbins.com/disc
- DiSC style descriptions — https://www.discprofile.com/disc-styles
- Gallup's four CliftonStrengths domains — https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/253736/cliftonstrengths-domains.aspx
- Discussion of combining strengths and DiSC frameworks — https://boarcommunity.com/combining-clifton-strengthsfinder-and-wiley-everything-disc-workplace/

The application does not call these sites, retrieve their assessments, use their proprietary questions, or claim to produce their official scores. It uses six original behavioral-preference questions and six original questions organized around broad contribution domains. The output is an informal development reflection, not a validated psychometric instrument.

The previously supplied Strawberry.me archetypes URL was not independently verifiable during implementation. The five-question coach matcher therefore uses four original interaction preferences (Action-oriented, Connector, Reflective, and Analytical); it does not claim to reproduce Strawberry.me's framework.

## Coaching foundation

The coaching instructions are informed by the broad management practices identified in the PRD and by the user's stated interest in *The Effective Manager*. The app does not reproduce the book's text. The AI coach can use administrator-uploaded, authorized documents as supporting context. External publisher links are passed as short curated signposts when relevant, not as full-text sources.

## External source handling

- External resource summaries are written in original language and point to the publisher's page.
- Full publisher articles are not copied into the app or the Gemini retrieval corpus.
- Admin-uploaded PDFs and text documents remain a separate private knowledge source, subject to the document's access tier and storage rules.
- Do not upload copyrighted or confidential documents without permission.
