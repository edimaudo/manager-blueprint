Coaching archetypes

Your answers identify the coaching approach that is most useful to you. The archetype adjusts tone, prompts and pace; it does not limit the coach's capabilities.

Action-oriented

Direct challenge, clear priorities, concrete next steps and accountability.

Connector

Collaborative exploration, encouragement, influence and relationship-building.

Reflective

Thoughtful questions, trust, considered pacing and space to process complexity.

Analytical

Evidence-led reasoning, structured options, decision criteria and checkpoints.
